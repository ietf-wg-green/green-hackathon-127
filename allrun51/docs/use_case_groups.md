# Use-Case Groups

## Group A: Event-Ready

`allrun51` implements these 14 use cases in numeric order:

1. UC 2.1 — Incremental Application of the GREEN Framework
2. UC 2.2 — Selective reduction of energy consumption in network parts proportional to traffic levels
3. UC 2.5 — Indirect Energy Monitoring and control
4. UC 2.6 — Consideration of other domains for obtention of end-to-end metrics
5. UC 2.7 — Dynamic adjustment of network element throughput in wireless transport networks
6. UC 2.8 — Video streaming use case
7. UC 2.9 — WLAN Network Energy Saving
8. UC 2.10 — Fixed Network Energy Saving
9. UC 2.11 — Energy Efficiency Network Management
10. UC 2.12 — ISAC-enabled Energy-Aware Smart City Traffic Management
11. UC 2.13 — Double Accounting Open issue
12. UC 2.14 — Energy Efficiency Under Power Shortage
13. UC 2.15 — Energy-Efficient Management of Distributed AI Training Workloads
14. UC 2.16 — Network-level Cross Layer Energy Saving

Each event and description is a deterministic simulator representation, not an IETF-standard event name. New use cases are grounded in the upstream [GREEN use-case draft](https://github.com/emile22/draft-ietf-green-use-cases/blob/main/draft-ietf-green-use-cases.md).

Group A contains 122 deterministic events. The doubled per-use-case minimums are UC 2.1: 8; UC 2.2: 10; UC 2.5: 8; UC 2.6: 8; UC 2.7: 8; UC 2.8: 8; UC 2.9: 10; UC 2.10: 8; UC 2.11: 8; UC 2.12: 10; UC 2.13: 8; UC 2.14: 10; UC 2.15: 8; UC 2.16: 10.
